Privacy Policy

Operational data

KEA Operations stores account credentials, clock-in telemetry, operational messages, submitted files, profile images, and audit events so authorized teams can coordinate field work.

Access control

Role-based sessions restrict each user to their assigned workspace. Private chat messages and attachments are available only to the two roles in that conversation.

Retention

Operational records are retained according to the organization’s internal retention schedule. Contact the Super Admin team to request correction or access support.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sessions use HttpOnly cookies. Uploaded files are size-limited and served only to authorized participants.
